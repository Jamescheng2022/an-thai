About An-Thai Engineering

We are a leading engineering firm dedicated to delivering high-quality solutions with integrity and innovation across Thailand and Southeast Asia.

Our Story

Founded in 2005, An-Thai Engineering has grown from a small consultancy into one of Thailand's most respected engineering firms. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide engineering services that combine technical excellence with ethical practice.

Over the years, we have completed hundreds of successful projects across Thailand and neighboring countries, building a reputation for reliability, innovation, and safety. Our team has expanded to include specialists in structural engineering, geotechnical analysis, construction management, and environmental compliance.

Today, we continue to push the boundaries of what's possible in engineering, embracing new technologies and sustainable practices while maintaining our commitment to quality and integrity in everything we do.

Our Values

Integrity: We believe in honesty, transparency, and ethical practice in all our dealings. Our clients trust us because we deliver on our promises.

Excellence: We strive for technical perfection in every project, constantly updating our knowledge and skills to deliver the best solutions.

Innovation: We embrace new technologies and methodologies, finding creative solutions to complex engineering challenges.

Safety: The safety of our designs, construction sites, and end-users is paramount in everything we do.

Sustainability: We design with the future in mind, minimizing environmental impact and creating structures that stand the test of time.
