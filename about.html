Company Profile · AN-THAI Engineering

AN-THAI Engineering Company Limited — Bangkok-based technical practice operating four independent business units with clear responsibility boundaries.

01 · Identity & Presence

Who We Are

AN-THAI Engineering Company Limited is an independent engineering technical practice incorporated in Bangkok, Thailand (1339 Arwan Building, Pracharat 1 Rd, Wongsawang, Bangsue District, Bangkok 10800). We operate across the Greater Bangkok Metropolitan Area, the Eastern Economic Corridor (EEC / Rayong / Chonburi), and major Thai industrial corridors.

Backed by over 40 years of heavy civil and foundation engineering heritage through our strategic group association with Pathumthani Concrete (PACO, founded 1978, pioneer of TIS 398-2563 prestressed spun piles Ø250–800 mm, JIP jack-in pile systems introduced with CSCG Singapore, and FC-PTG copyright registration No. 8251, with dual manufacturing bases in Lad Lum Kaeo and Rayong EEC) and Roge & Associates (founded 1983, Thai Ministry of Finance Grade A Consultant with 105+ staff and licensed Thai PEs), AN-THAI bridges international standards (ASTM, BS, EN, ISO) with local Thai regulatory and engineering codes (TIS, EIT, BMA).

03 · Execution Workflow

Engineering Delivery Chain

Every assignment follows a rigorous, stage-gated delivery chain ensuring technical compliance, data traceability, and contractual certainty from briefing to handover.

STEP 01

Brief & Specs Lock

IFC drawings, geotechnical soil logs, pile schedules, material specifications, and project timeline confirmation.

STEP 02

Technical Method Review

Code reconciliation (ASTM / BS / TIS / EIT), test plan drafting, reaction frame calculation, and statutory PE review requirement check.

STEP 03

Field / Shop Execution

Calibrated equipment mobilization, sensor setup, certified technician operation, raw waveform/curve acquisition, and witness sign-off.

STEP 04

QA & Report Handover

Data cross-validation, CAPWAP / velocity analysis, MTC traceability ledger, formal engineering report, and licensed PE stamp where contracted.
