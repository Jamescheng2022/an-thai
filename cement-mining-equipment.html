Cement & Mining Process Equipment

Technical coordination and supply support for cement and mining process systems—crushing, grinding, pyro-processing peripherals, air classification, conveying, electrical control, commissioning coordination, and spares—backed by qualified OEMs.

Illustrative engineering visualization · Representative industrial scope imagery, not a claimed AN-THAI jobsite photograph.

Crushing, Grinding & Classification

Raw material crushing and pre-homogenization, mining crushing and screening stations, raw meal and cement grinding systems (VRM, ball mill, roller press), plus dynamic air separators and baghouse dust collectors.

Pyro Peripherals, Conveying & Spares

Clinker pyro-processing system peripherals, bulk conveying and storage handling equipment, and wear-resistant critical spare parts matched to plant throughput and layout.

Control, Commissioning & OEM Boundary

Electrical control cabinet supply coordination and OEM-supported process commissioning guidance with qualified cement and mining OEMs. Excludes civil works, MEP erection general contracting, and EPC; specs and warranties are confirmed directly by the OEM.

You Receive

Process duty parameter sheet, preliminary circuit and unit sizing summary, electrical control interface checklist, OEM-confirmed supply scope, and spare parts schedule.

Role Clarification

AN-THAI provides project-duty technical coordination and OEM supply alignment in Thailand—covering equipment coordination and OEM-supported commissioning guidance only, not local civil works, MEP erection general contracting, or EPC turnkey contracting. Final equipment specifications and warranties are issued by the qualified OEM per project.

Before You Contact Us (Sizing Inputs)+
Raw material / ore type & moisture · Feed lump size & target product fineness or gradation · Target throughput (t/h or t/d) · Process stage (crushing/screening, VRM/ball mill/roller press, pyro peripherals, conveying/storage, electrical control, or spares) · Power supply & destination port.
