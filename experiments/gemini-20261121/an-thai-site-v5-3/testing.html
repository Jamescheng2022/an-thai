Foundation Testing & Geotech

Pile load testing, integrity screening and geotechnical investigation in Thailand, with traceable field data and project-specific engineering reports.

Load Testing (Static / PDA)

Static load testing (kentledge or reaction frame) per approved plan, plus High-Strain Dynamic Testing (PDA) for bearing capacity estimation under suitable test conditions.

Pile Integrity (PIT / CSL)

Low-strain PIT screening and Crosshole Sonic Logging (CSL) evaluating concrete acoustic uniformity between access tube paths alongside construction records.

Ground Investigation

Borehole drilling, soil sampling, and laboratory soil testing coordinated around foundation design inputs.

Monitoring & Engineering Support

Field settlement/structural monitoring and Thai drawing/submission coordination where contracted.

Field Testing Workflow

You Receive

Approved test method/plan, raw field measurement data, load-settlement curves or waveforms, engineering interpretation report, and licensed Thai engineer sign-off where contracted.

Responsibility Boundary

Test methods, acceptance criteria, and statutory sign-off follow project specifications and licensed Thai professional responsibility confirmed per contract.

Before You Contact Us (Required Inputs)+
Project location/province · Pile type, diameter, length & test count · Design working load & required test load · Construction schedule · Drawings & soil profile logs.
