Pile Testing

Foundation performance & integrity

Testing methods are selected according to pile type, project objectives, loading requirements and available access.

Static Load Test · 静载试验

  • Reaction system setup and staged loading
  • Settlement measurement and load–settlement curve
  • Compression, uplift or lateral arrangements according to project requirements
  • Engineering interpretation and test report

High-Strain Dynamic Pile Test · 高应变动力试验

  • Impact loading at pile head
  • Force and velocity measurement
  • Wave-equation based analysis
  • Capacity and integrity assessment

Low-Strain Integrity Test · 低应变完整性检测

  • Pile-head preparation and light hammer impact
  • Accelerometer signal collection
  • Reflected-wave interpretation
  • Rapid integrity screening and anomaly indication

Crosshole Sonic Logging (CSL) · 声波透射法

  • For cast-in-place bored piles with pre-installed access tubes
  • Transmitter and receiver probes in water-filled tubes
  • Travel-time and signal-strength profiles
  • Anomaly zone identification and integrity interpretation
