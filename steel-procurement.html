07 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before the first quote.

Each project is different. These are the questions worth answering early.

Can we use our existing steel fabricator?

Yes. AN-THAI can scope technical coordination, fabrication follow-up or inspection support independently of sourcing a new manufacturer.

Do you provide Tekla or shop drawings?

Shop detailing can be arranged or coordinated through specialist resources where suitable. Design responsibility, required approvals, deliverables, revisions and fees are agreed project by project.

Can we request inspection without a sourcing service?

Yes. We can discuss inspection planning and coordination, including suitable independent third-party providers. Accredited reports are issued by the responsible inspection or testing body, not automatically by AN-THAI.

Do you handle erection or installation?

The primary offering is off-site fabrication supply and coordination. On-site installation is outside the standard scope.
