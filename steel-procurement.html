Buyer-Side Steel Procurement Agency

Independent sourcing and procurement coordination for international buyers: supplier shortlisting, comparable RFQs, factory verification and pre-shipment review.

Scope & RFQ

Review IFC drawings, BOQ, steel grades, welding specs, and coating exclusions to issue a unified RFQ package.

Supplier Search & Comparison

Shortlist qualified fabricators, audit real shop capacity, and normalize FOB/CIF/DDP quotes for like-for-like comparison.

Production QA & Traceability

Verify mill test certificates (MTC), heat numbers, welding QC, trial assembly, and coating dry-film thickness.

Shipment Verification

Witness dimensional checks, final NDT, export packing lists, and issue pre-shipment review dossier.

Buyer-Side Procurement Agency Flow

You Receive

Independence & Commercial Boundary

AN-THAI acts exclusively for the buyer with transparent fees and zero hidden factory commissions. Final fabricator selection, commercial contract, and structural design approval remain with the buyer.
