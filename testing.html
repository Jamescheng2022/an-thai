Foundation Testing & Geotech

Pile load testing, integrity screening and geotechnical investigation in Thailand, with traceable field data and project-specific engineering reports.

ASTM D1143 · DIRECT CAPACITY

Static Load Testing (SLT)

Definitive axial compressive, uplift (ASTM D3689), or lateral (ASTM D3966) pile capacity determination using calibrated hydraulic jacks and reaction kentledge/frames. Provides real load-settlement curves and Davisson failure criterion verification.

ASTM D4945 · DYNAMIC

High-Strain Dynamic Testing (PDA)

Accelerometers and strain transducers under drop-hammer impact. CAPWAP signal-matching numerical analysis delivers dynamic soil resistance and estimated static capacity alongside driving stress monitoring.

ASTM D5882 · INTEGRITY ONLY

Low-Strain Integrity Testing (PIT)

Sonic echo / transient response screening with calibrated impact hammer and motion transducer. Evaluates pile shaft continuity, length, and necking/voids. Note: PIT is strictly an integrity screening method, not a direct bearing capacity measurement.

ASTM D6760 · ULTRASONIC

Crosshole Sonic Logging (CSL)

Multi-channel acoustic transmission across water-filled access tubes in deep bored piles and diaphragm walls. Generates full-depth acoustic velocity profiles to distinguish genuine concrete flaws from tube debonding anomalies.

ASTM D1586 · SOIL DRILLING

Geotechnical Ground Investigation

Borehole drilling (rotary wash), Standard Penetration Testing (SPT), undisturbed Shelby tube sampling, groundwater observation, and accredited laboratory soil mechanics testing for foundation engineering design.

INSTRUMENTATION · SAFETY

Excavation & Structural Monitoring

Real-time geotechnical and structural monitoring: inclinometers for diaphragm wall lateral movement, vibrating-wire piezometers, settlement arrays, tilt sensors, and adjacent vibration monitoring with alarm triggers.

CROSS-CUTTING ENGINEERING SERVICE

Thai Drawing Conversion, PE Review & Statutory Submission Coordination

Converting international structural and geotechnical engineering drawings into Thai statutory formats (EIT / TIS codes). Licensed Thai Professional Engineers (Senior / Professional level) review structural calculations, recalculate local seismic and wind loads, and coordinate building permit (Or.1) technical documentation with municipal authorities, BMA, and IEAT industrial estates.
